# Quickstart: FEAT-10 — Ownership Transfer

1. As a task owner, open `TaskDetailScreen`.
2. Tap the popup menu (three dots) or "Sahipliği Devret" button.
3. Enter the recipient user's email address and submit.
4. Verify request creation toast.
5. As the recipient user, tap the "Devir İstekleri" icon on the Home/Tasks screen (with badge count).
6. View the incoming transfer request card with task title and sender.
7. Tap "Kabul Et" (or "Reddet").
8. Verify success toast and observe the task transferred to your tasks list!
